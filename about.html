About Surface

Surface helps small businesses build websites and web apps that are simple, fast, and easy to use. We’re a small team of three frontend-focused developers who care about how things feel on the web, not just how they look. We focus on clarity, performance, and thoughtful design that helps people understand your business without friction.

We believe in clean layouts, smooth interactions, and building only what you actually need. No bloated features, no overcomplication. Because we’re a small team, the work stays personal. You’ll work directly with us from start to finish, with clear communication and no handoffs.
